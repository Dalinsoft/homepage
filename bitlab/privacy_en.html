🛡️ MyBit - Upbit Simulator Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

MyBit - Upbit Simulator (operated by Dalin Soft, the "App") values your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Summary
· The App has no sign-up and no login. It does not collect or store your email, user ID, or similar information on any developer server (the developer does not operate a server for this App).
· Your strategies, virtual orders and fills, virtual balance, and backtest results are stored only on your device.
· The App does not ask for or store any exchange API key, and does not send real orders.
· Google AdMob processes some information to show rewarded ads.

1. Information the App handles

(1) Account / login

ItemsNone
NoteThe App has no login feature.

(2) Usage records stored on your device

Items (examples) Strategies you set up, virtual orders/fills/balance/holdings, trade logs, notification records, backtest (simulation) results, app lock setting, and records of how many times you used ad rewards
WhereCreated while you use the App and stored only on your device. Not sent anywhere.
PurposeTo provide paper trading, show your results and records, and provide reports

(3) Upbit-related information

ItemsNone
ImportantThe App does not ask for or store an Upbit API key (Access Key, Secret Key) and does not send real orders.
NoteThe App fetches public market data (current prices, candles, etc.) that Upbit provides over the internet. Ordinary network information (such as your IP address) may be passed to Upbit's servers in the process.

(4) App lock (device authentication)

How it worksIf you turn on the app lock, the App calls your device's authentication (fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, etc.). The operating system handles the authentication. The App does not read or store your biometric data or PIN; it only learns whether authentication succeeded.

(5) Advertising identifiers (ad SDK)

Items (examples) Advertising ID, device and event information, IP address, approximate location, etc. — processed by the Google AdMob SDK to deliver ads
Processed byGoogle LLC (Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads)
PurposeShowing rewarded ads to keep the App free, measuring ad performance, and preventing fraud
Note The App itself does not separately collect or store this information; the scope follows Google's policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

1-2. Permissions

The App uses the permissions below. Notifications are optional, and the main features work even if you do not allow them.

※ The App does not request location, contacts, phone, SMS, photos, camera, or microphone permissions, and it has no sign-up or login, so it does not ask for account information.

2. Purpose of use

Usage records you keep on your device are processed only on your device to provide the App's features. Advertising identifiers and similar data are processed through Google AdMob to deliver rewarded ads that keep the App free.

3. Retention

4. Deletion

The App does not store your information on any developer server. Records on your device are removed when you uninstall the App, and some can be removed with the App's reset features (such as resetting the virtual balance).

5. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not provide your information to third parties. However, Google AdMob processes the information in section 1 (5) when ads are shown.

8. Your rights

Because the App does not store your information on any developer server, the developer has no information to look up, correct, or delete for you. You can remove the records on your device by uninstalling the App or using its reset features, and you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

9. Security

The App does not store your information on any developer server. To protect the records on your device from loss or theft, we recommend using your device lock screen, installing OS security updates, and turning on the App's app lock.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy is changed, we will announce it in the App or through an official channel at least 7 days before the change takes effect.
