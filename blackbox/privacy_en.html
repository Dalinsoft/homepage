📹 DashCaptain Privacy Policy

Effective: July 10, 2026 · Last updated: September 2, 2026

DashCaptain (operated by Dalin Soft, the "App") takes your privacy seriously and complies with applicable data protection laws, including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the App processes and how.

Summary
1) The App has no sign-up or login, and does not collect or transmit your recordings or app settings to any operator server.
2) Video and audio captured by the camera and microphone are stored only on your device.
3) To keep the Service free, it uses Google AdMob ads, and some device and usage information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners while ads are delivered.
4) In the European Economic Area, the UK, and other applicable regions, an ad privacy choices screen is shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the app.
5) A clip is only transferred to an external app you choose when you actively initiate the sharing feature yourself.

3. Purpose of processing

The App processes on-device information to record video and audio, store/play/manage clips, keep your settings, and provide the external sharing feature you request. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process advertising-related information to show ads that keep the Service free, measure ad performance and analytics, and prevent fraud and maintain security.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Completed recording clips are kept in the app's dedicated storage area on your device.
  • Clips may be automatically deleted, oldest first, under the app's rolling retention policy, and you can also delete them yourself in the app.
  • App settings and recordings are deleted according to your device and operating system's behavior when app data or the app itself is removed.
  • Users should export important footage to another safe location themselves before it is automatically deleted.
  • Retention and deletion of advertising-related information follow Google's privacy policy and ad policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

5. Sharing with third parties and external SDKs

The app operator does not provide your recordings or app settings to any third party at its own discretion. However, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect advertising-related information while delivering ads and share it with Google and its advertising partners. Also, when a user actively initiates clip sharing, the selected file is transferred to the external app the user chose through Android's sharing feature, after which that external service's terms and privacy policy apply.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

Because the app operator does not keep your recordings or app settings on a server, there is no server-side data for the operator to look up, correct, or delete on your behalf. You can manage information on your device using the app's own deletion features, by clearing app data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings, and manage personalized ads in Google's Ad Settings. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and other applicable regions can choose whether to consent on the ad privacy choices screen shown the first time they run the app, and can review or change that choice at any time afterward in Ad privacy settings inside the app. Note that even if you restrict personalized ads, some information needed for ad delivery, measurement, and security may still be processed.

8. Security measures

  • The App does not transmit recordings to any operator server; they are kept in the app's dedicated storage area.
  • External apps are granted access only to the specific file a user chooses to share, and only for that purpose.
  • Advertising-related information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.
  • Users should maintain their own device security, such as screen lock, OS security updates, and preventing device loss.

9. Children's privacy

The App does not verify users' ages or intentionally collect personal information from children. Guardians should make sure that children using the recording or audio features comply with applicable law and the rights of others.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date and the content of the change will be announced in the app or on the official web page at least 7 days before it takes effect. If a change materially affects users' rights, it will be announced in the manner and within the period required by applicable law.
