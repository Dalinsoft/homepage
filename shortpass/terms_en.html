1. Purpose

These terms set out the rights, obligations, and responsibilities between the service and users when users use ShortPass (operated by Dalin Soft, the "Service") as a helper tool that automatically swipes to the next video when a YouTube Short ends.

2. Definitions

  • "Service" means the mobile application that detects the moment a Short ends in the YouTube app and automatically swipes to the next video.
  • "Short" means the short vertical video screen provided by the YouTube app.
  • "Usage time" means the time during which the Service's auto-advance can be used, made up of free time and passes.
  • "Free time" means 10 minutes provided every day, refilled when the date changes (midnight by the device clock) and not accumulated.
  • "Pass" means 3 hours of usage time granted when a user watches a rewarded ad to the end; passes stack when earned more than once.
  • "Accessibility service" means an Android feature that runs only if the user turns it on in the device settings.
  • "User" means a person who agrees to these terms and uses the Service.

4. Account / login

  • The Service does not provide sign-up or login.
  • Usage time and settings are stored on the user's device.

5. Provision and changes of the Service

1) Features

  • Detects the moment a YouTube Short ends and swipes up to the next video automatically
  • Skips ads, image posts, and live-stream screens automatically (can be turned on or off in settings)
  • Shows a "Start" button over YouTube (left or right position selectable in settings)
  • Auto-stop time setting and display of remaining usage time

2) Free service, usage time, and ads

  • The Service is free, and shows ads (rewarded ads) to cover operating costs.
  • 10 minutes of free time is provided every day. While you have no pass, free time decreases by the time auto-advance is used.
  • When a user taps "Watch ad", watches a rewarded ad to the end, and closes it, a 3-hour pass is granted. A pass runs down with the clock whether or not you are watching Shorts.
  • If an ad cannot be loaded or shown, or the ad is not watched to the end, a pass may not be granted. However, if the ad provider responds that there is no ad to show, a pass may be granted without an ad.
  • Usage time is not a paid product, and the Service offers no in-app purchases. A pass cannot be transferred or exchanged for anything else.
  • Usage time is calculated by the device clock, so users must not manipulate the clock to gain usage time unfairly.

The Service may change all or part of the Service as operationally necessary.

8. Accessibility service

  • The Service's auto-advance uses the Android accessibility service and works only if the user turns it on in the device settings. The Service explains what it reads before it is turned on.
  • The accessibility service reads, limited to the YouTube app's screen, the playback position of a Short, ad / image-post / live-stream markers, and on-screen text used to tell whether the video has changed. It does not read other apps' screens, anything the user types, passwords, or messages.
  • What it reads is used only to decide when to swipe and is neither stored nor transmitted.
  • The accessibility service is used for the upward swipe gesture and the "Start" button over YouTube, and it does not record or capture the screen.
  • Users can turn the accessibility service off at any time in the device's accessibility menu; when it is off, auto-advance cannot be used.
