⏭️ ShortPass Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

ShortPass (operated by Dalin Soft, the "App") takes your privacy seriously and complies with applicable data protection laws, including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the App processes and how.

Summary
1) The App has no sign-up or login, and the operator does not collect or store your email or account information.
2) Your remaining usage time and settings are stored only on your device.
3) Using an accessibility service that you turn on yourself, the App reads only the playback position of a Short and the ad / image-post / live-stream markers on the YouTube app's screen. What it reads is not stored and not transmitted. The App does not collect your YouTube account, watch history, comments, or searches, and does not read other apps' screens.
4) The App does not request location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions. However, the Google ads SDK may estimate your approximate region from your IP address.
5) To stay free, the App uses Google AdMob ads. Watching an ad to the end adds usage time. The Google ads SDK may process your IP address, interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
6) In the European Economic Area, the UK, and other applicable regions, an ad privacy choices screen is shown, and you can change your choice at any time on the App's info screen.

3. Purpose of processing

The operator does not directly collect or use personal information in principle. Your remaining usage time and settings are processed only on your device to provide the App's features, and the YouTube screen information read through the accessibility service is used only to swipe Shorts and is not stored. Ad-related information is processed through Google AdMob for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention to keep the App free, and your privacy choice status may be processed through Google UMP to comply with applicable laws and to check whether ads may be requested.

That processing follows Google's policies and your applicable privacy choices.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

The operator does not directly collect or store personal information, so there is in principle nothing held by the operator to access, correct, or delete. You can remove the usage time and settings stored on your device by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in the device settings.

The accessibility service stops immediately when you turn ShortPass off in the device's accessibility menu. Where a privacy choices menu is required in your region, you can manage your choice again under "Ad privacy choices" on the App's info screen, and in Android's ad settings you can reset or delete your advertising ID or adjust the ad privacy choices provided. To exercise rights over information processed by Google, you can use the Google privacy contact form.

8. Security measures

The operator does not collect or store personal information directly. We recommend keeping your device secure (screen lock, OS security updates, etc.) so that the usage time and settings stored on it are not lost, stolen, or leaked. Information sent by Google SDKs is encrypted with TLS, and the security measures of external services follow Google's policies.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children, and the operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
